# Sensitivity analysis

Generated by `python scripts/sensitivity.py`. 56 journeys (all ordered pairs of the 8 named places), departure 22:30 on 14 Nov 2026 (Kensington Gardens closed, Hyde Park open). No overlay applied.

## 1. What preference strength buys

`lambda` scales all condition weights together. `lambda = 0` is the fastest route.

| lambda | mean extra time | max extra time | mean time unlit (obs. or expected) | mean time off-street | mean share recorded lit | routes differing from fastest |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0 s (0.0%) | 0.0 min | 113 s | 166 s | 75.2% | 0% |
| 0.25 | 11 s (0.6%) | 1.1 min | 27 s | 129 s | 84.1% | 79% |
| 0.5 | 38 s (2.1%) | 4.9 min | 4 s | 76 s | 87.9% | 86% |
| 1 | 67 s (3.5%) | 6.7 min | 4 s | 19 s | 89.4% | 86% |
| 2 | 80 s (4.4%) | 7.3 min | 3 s | 12 s | 92.2% | 93% |
| 4 | 166 s (8.7%) | 13.8 min | 1 s | 12 s | 96.2% | 96% |

Reading: at the default `lambda = 1` the average journey is 67 s (3.5%) longer than the fastest route and spends 97% less time on segments that are unlit or expected to be, and 89% less time away from any street. Quadrupling the preference to `lambda = 4` adds a further 99 s on average for a further 3% of the original unlit time: most of the available improvement is already taken at the default.

## 2. Robustness to each weight

Each weight is halved and doubled in turn with the others held at their defaults. "Overlap" is corridor overlap with the default recommendation: the share of route length running within 30 m of the other route, so walking on the opposite pavement of the same street counts as the same route. "Same corridor" is the share of journeys with overlap of at least 90%.

| weight | default | scaled | same corridor | mean overlap | worst overlap | mean abs. time change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| unlit | 1 | x0.5 | 100% | 99.9% | 98% | 1 s |
| unlit | 1 | x2 | 96% | 96.7% | 12% | 1 s |
| isolated | 0.5 | x0.5 | 93% | 96.5% | 47% | 27 s |
| isolated | 0.5 | x2 | 100% | 100.0% | 100% | 4 s |
| enclosed | 1 | x0.5 | 100% | 100.0% | 100% | 1 s |
| enclosed | 1 | x2 | 100% | 100.0% | 100% | 0 s |
| quiet | 0.2 | x0.5 | 89% | 93.7% | 12% | 4 s |
| quiet | 0.2 | x2 | 82% | 88.1% | 6% | 7 s |

Reading: averaged over the eight perturbations, 95% of journeys keep the same corridor. The recommendation is least stable under `quiet` x2 (82% same corridor, 88% mean overlap, worst single journey 6%). Time differences stay small (largest mean change 27 s) because the alternatives are near-ties in walking time: a 2x error in any single weight moves some journeys onto a parallel street, but does not produce long detours.

## 3. Dependence on unrecorded lighting

Lighting is recorded on 61% of network length. For the rest we use P(unlit | street group) estimated from recorded segments in the same area:

| street group | P(unlit) used | recorded km | unrecorded km | coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| main_road | 0.013 | 132.68 | 58.88 | 69% |
| local_street | 0.009 | 136.79 | 97.04 | 58% |
| park | 0.570 | 53.21 | 28.54 | 65% |
| off_street | 0.267 | 16.37 | 26.98 | 38% |
| service | 0.055 | 1.12 | 9.46 | 11% |

Rerunning with every unrecorded segment assumed lit (0) and assumed unlit (1):

| assumption for unrecorded segments | same corridor | mean overlap | worst overlap | mean abs. time change | mean share of route unrecorded |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| all lit (0) | 96% | 97.3% | 39% | 4 s | 13.3% |
| group estimate (default) | 100% | 100.0% | 100% | 0 s | 10.5% |
| all unlit (1) | 68% | 86.3% | 10% | 37 s | 1.3% |

Reading: this is the largest source of uncertainty in the model. Assuming every unrecorded segment is unlit keeps 68% of journeys in the same corridor (mean overlap 86%) and costs 37 s on average; the routes that move go onto streets with recorded lighting (unrecorded share falls from 10.5% to 1.3%). Assuming they are all lit keeps 96%. We therefore never present unrecorded lighting as lit: the app shows the unrecorded share of every route next to the lit share.
